About

I am an Aerospace Engineer and Doctoral Candidate with a background in computational fluid dynamics (CFD), hypersonic flows, and aerodynamic shape optimization. I completed my M.Sc. in Aerospace Engineering at Middle East Technical University (METU), where my research focused on mitigating ignition overpressure in rocket launch pads using unsteady adjoint-based shape optimization with the open-source SU2 solver.

From 2024 to 2026, I worked as a Project Assistant at METU on a TÜBİTAK ARDEB-1001 project, investigating pressure wave propagation and launch-pad design optimization. Previously, I earned my B.Sc. from Samsun University and completed an Erasmus+ research internship at Politecnico di Milano, studying hypersonic flows around atmospheric re-entry vehicles using SU2-NEMO.

Outside my research, I enjoy playing electric guitar and exploring the night sky through amateur astronomy. I am also passionate about history and archaeology, and I enjoy visiting museums and archaeological sites to learn more about past civilizations and human history.
