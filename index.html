About

I'm Ercan Umut Danışan, an Aerospace Engineer and Doctoral Candidate. I completed my M.Sc. at Middle East Technical University (METU) under the supervision of Prof. Dr. Sinan Eyi, where my thesis focused on Ignition Overpressure (IOP) mitigation in launch pads using unsteady adjoint-based shape optimization, developed with the open-source SU2 CFD solver.

From May 2024 to October 2026, throughout my M.Sc., I worked as an Aerospace Engineer (Project Assistant) on the TUBITAK 1001-ARDEB project "Development of Flow Analysis and Design Optimization Software for Launch Pads" (#123M355), where I investigated pressure wave propagation in launch environments and design optimization methodologies for pad geometries. My broader research interests include computational fluid dynamics on unstructured grids, hypersonic flows, turbulence modeling, and high-performance computing.

Prior to my M.Sc., I earned a B.Sc. in Aerospace Engineering from Samsun University and completed an Erasmus+ research internship at Politecnico di Milano, where I worked on hypersonic flow analysis of re-entry vehicles using SU2-NEMO.
