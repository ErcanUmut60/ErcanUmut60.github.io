I picked up the electric guitar in 2018, during my English prep year at the start of university, and I have been playing ever since. I love headbanging to heavy tunes, but I enjoy the slower, quieter songs just as much.

Styles & Repertoire

Rock · Hard rock · Heavy metal · Thrash metal

AC/DC is my favorite rock band and one of the main reasons I got into the electric guitar in the first place. I am also a big Metallica fan, both of their ballads and of their heavy riffs. What I love most about Metallica is the depth behind their songs: there is often a deeper meaning buried in the lyrics, and their grasp of culture and history shines through in their music.
